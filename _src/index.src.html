How it works

  1. They march.

    Plodders walk on their own and never stop – not at cliffs, not at lava, not at anything.

  2. You hand out jobs.

    Pause, tap a plodder, pick a skill. Build stairs, punch through rock, drill down, glide over the abyss.

  3. Bring them home.

    Save enough to clear the level. Save every single one for three stars.

8 skills. Endless combinations.

Every level hands you a limited set. Deciding who does what – and exactly when – is the whole puzzle.

1000 levels. 34 worlds. One stubborn crowd.

Quarry, foundry, frozen caverns, sunken ruins and the clock tower – then all of them again by moonlight, at twilight, toxic, crimson, stormy and golden. The first levels teach you gently. The expert levels will make you sweat.

Fair by design

No forced ads

An ad only plays if you choose to watch one – for a boost after a failed level. Say no and play on.

No in-app purchases

All 1000 levels are free. No gems, no energy bars, no waiting timers.

Plays offline

No account, no login, no internet connection needed to play.

Pause, rewind, fast-forward

Plan every rescue calmly – or undo a mistake in a second.
